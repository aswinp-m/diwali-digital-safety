When something feels suspicious

Two things to do at home

Official credit freeze pages

If you sent money or shared a code

Call your bank or payment service right away using its official number and ask whether it can stop the payment. If someone accessed your account, change your password and sign out other devices. Save messages and receipts.

Tell someone you trust. You don’t have to handle it alone.

  • Report a scam to the FTC
