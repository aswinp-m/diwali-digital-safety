If you sent money or shared a code

Tell someone you trust. You don’t have to sort this out alone. Call your bank or payment service right away using the number on your card or in the app, and ask if it can stop the payment. If someone got into your account, open the service’s official recovery page, change your password and sign out other devices. Keep the messages and receipts.
