When a call or message feels urgent

Protect accounts and credit at home

If you sent money or shared a code

Call your bank or payment service immediately using its official number. Ask whether it can stop or reverse the payment. For a compromised account, use its official recovery page, change the password and sign out other sessions. Save messages and receipts.
